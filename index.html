01

FloodGuard

Hyperlocal intelligence for flash-flood prediction, risk & response

FloodGuard is a hyperlocal, AI-powered flash-flood prediction, risk analysis and response platform, built for a Smart India Hackathon 2026 problem statement on flash-flood prediction for hilly regions (Wayanad, Kerala). It combines rainfall, environmental, terrain and soil data with machine-learning models to turn live conditions into street-level risk and actionable warnings.

  • ML-based flood-risk prediction from live rainfall, terrain, soil and land-cover data
  • Risk visualisation at 30 m micro-zone scale on a 3D digital twin
  • Real-time monitoring with geofenced alerts for people inside the danger area
  • Disaster response: evacuation routing and live rescue tracking
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • FastAPI
  • PostGIS
  • XGBoost

Indian Sign Language Detection

AI & computer vision for accessible communication

3rd Prize — Bureau of Indian Standards Indian Sign Language Detection challenge

An AI and computer-vision application that recognises Indian Sign Language (ISL) gestures in real time and converts them into understandable text and speech — forming natural sentences from continuous gestures with an accessibility-first design.

  • Real-time hand detection and gesture recognition from a webcam
  • Three modes: daily-life sentences, A–Z alphabet and numbers 0–20
  • Continuous sentence formation from multiple gestures
  • Multi-language text-to-speech in English, Hindi and Tamil
  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • MediaPipe
  • NumPy
  • Text-to-Speech
